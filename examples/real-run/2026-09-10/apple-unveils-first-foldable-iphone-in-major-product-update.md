# Apple unveils first foldable iPhone in major product update

*Category: technology · Sources: BBC World, Sky News World, ABC News US, The Hill, CNBC Top News, Fortune, Ars Technica, Wired*
*Status: published · Backend: local:qwen3:14b*
*Model confidence: 0.85*

Apple has unveiled its first foldable iPhone, the iPhone Duo, during a product launch event in California. The device, priced at $2,000 (or £1,999), marks the first major design change to the iPhone in nearly 20 years, according to BBC World. The event, headlined by Apple’s new CEO, John Ternus, who took over from Tim Cook on Sept. 1, featured the debut of the foldable smartphone alongside refreshed Pro models, as reported by CNBC Top News. According to Ars Technica, the iPhone Duo is expected to be available next month. The launch, described by The Hill as a significant moment for Apple, also included updates to the AirPods and Apple Watch. Fortune noted that this is the biggest iPhone update in years, with Ternus taking the stage for his first public appearance as CEO. The event, titled “Surprise and Shine,” highlighted Apple’s shift in product design and strategy under Ternus’s leadership.
